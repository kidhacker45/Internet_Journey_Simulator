        Real concept or simplification?

        Every step in the simulator carries a “real networking concept” note and a “simplified simulation” note. These are the general caveats behind them:

          This simulator models common Internet networking concepts. Real network paths, DNS responses, routing decisions, NAT behavior, transport protocols, and cloud infrastructure can vary.

          Network addresses and routes displayed in this simulation are illustrative. Nothing here is a real connection, traceroute, or lookup.
